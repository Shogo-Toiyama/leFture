- Do NOT produce a literal, word-for-word translation. A literal translation of English filler words, sentence rhythm, jokes, or idioms will sound obviously foreign and unnatural in ${LANGUAGE}.

Prioritize authenticity over fidelity to the original wording.
- Keep technical/academic terms in whatevever form a real ${LANGUAGE}-speaking professor would actually use them (e.g. many languages keep certain technical terms in English/loanword form rather than translating them — follow that real-world convention, do not force-translate terms that would normally stay as loanwords).
- Sentence length, rhythm, and register should feel natural for spoken ${LANGUAGE}, not mirror the English sentence structure.
