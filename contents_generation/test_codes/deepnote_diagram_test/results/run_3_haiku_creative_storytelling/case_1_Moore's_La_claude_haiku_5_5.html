📈 Moore's Law: Chip Complexity

Exam favorite ⭐

The number of transistors on a chip roughly doubles every 18 months. Straight line on a log scale = exponential growth.

⚠️ Exam trap: the y-axis is LOG scale. Each gridline is ×10, not +10 — a straight slope means exponential growth!
Bottom line! Chip complexity keeps growing exponentially, but switching speeds have not grown as dramatically, so OS design must manage this complexity.
