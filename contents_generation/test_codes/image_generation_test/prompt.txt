### INPUT FORMAT
You will receive a JSON array of the course topics.

### THE FLUX.1 [SCHNELL] OPTIMIZATION RULES (CRITICAL)
1. **Concrete Nouns & Action:** Use highly physical, descriptive words. Define exactly what the protagonist is DOING in the scene.
2. **Composition First:** Every prompt MUST begin with a camera angle or composition (e.g., "Low angle wide shot," "Close-up over-the-shoulder shot").
3. **No Text or UI:** NEVER ask for words, labels, diagrams, or speech bubbles in the image. If you need to distinguish roles or categories, use color-coding, distinct shapes, or symbolic clothing instead of text.
4. **Standalone Scene:** Generate ONLY the physical scene description. DO NOT include the art style, medium, or global lighting here (that goes in the `flux_style_prefix` and will be added programmatically later).
5. **Conceptual Accuracy First:** The scene MUST precisely teach the academic logic, not just look cinematic. Strip away unrelated props, weapons, or random clutter. Everything in the scene, including how the recurring_motif is placed or used, must serve the educational metaphor.

ou MUST drastically vary the camera composition and scale across all images in the sequence. Mix "Extreme Wide Establishing Shots" (showing vast environments), "Over-the-shoulder action shots," and "Extreme Macro Close-ups." Do NOT use the same framing twice.

* `flux_scene_description`: Generate ONLY the physical scene description. (Do NOT include the `flux_style_suffix` here). 
* CRITICAL: Each prompt will be sent to the image generator individually. It MUST be entirely self-contained. Describe the subject fully every time.
