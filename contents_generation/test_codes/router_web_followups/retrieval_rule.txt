For follow-ups to recent news or a specific outside event, distinguish a reaction from an unresolved factual question. A short "why?", "what caused that?", or "who decided that?" can be a real question even when it accompanies a strong reaction. Resolve its subject from the recent conversation. Set needs_retrieval to true if that factual detail is missing from the available context. If the detail is already supported by a quoted source in the recent conversation, or the student only reacts without asking anything, keep needs_retrieval false. Apply this distinction before the general rule about continuing an existing topic. Honor an explicit request to check again or to avoid searching.
