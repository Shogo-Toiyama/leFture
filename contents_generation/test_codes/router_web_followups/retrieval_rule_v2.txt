### SEARCH PRIORITY FOR FACTUAL FOLLOW-UPS
Before labeling a turn as chitchat, check whether it includes a question about an identifiable event in the recent conversation. When the student asks why an organization took a reported action, asks what triggered it, or asks for another missing factual detail, choose personal or learning and set needs_retrieval to true. A short standalone "Why?" inherits the action described in the previous turn, including when it sits between expressions of surprise or frustration. A quote that says an authority ordered an action establishes who ordered it. To answer why the authority took that action, look for the reason it gave. If the student asks only to restate or confirm a detail the prior reply already supports, or asks to avoid searching, set needs_retrieval to false. A request to verify independently or look up again requires retrieval. Treat a reaction with no factual question as chitchat.

Example: After "The transport authority closed the bridge yesterday," classify "Ugh, why?! That's so annoying" as a factual lookup. "Ugh, that's so annoying" is a reaction. If the previous reply already explained the closure and the student just asks to summarize that explanation, do not fetch again.
